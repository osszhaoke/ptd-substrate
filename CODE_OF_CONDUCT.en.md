# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)


## Working together
Respect privacy and differences in background. Discuss reproducible behaviour and address the work itself. Harassment, discrimination, threats and sharing personal information without consent are prohibited.

## Moderation
Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Report what happened with the minimum evidence needed. Use a [private channel](SECURITY.en.md) for sensitive details.
