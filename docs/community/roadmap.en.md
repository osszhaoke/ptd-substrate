# Roadmap

[Read in Chinese](roadmap.md)

The maintainer has chosen four priorities for the next version. The scope and timing may change. Planned items have a direction but no release date. Exploratory ideas may never ship.

## Next version

| Priority | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Signing and notarization | The aim is for your Mac to identify the publisher when you first open PTDS. |
| Local encryption | The goal is to protect health data stored on your Mac if someone obtains a copy of the files. |
| English throughout the app | More messages generated by the app will follow your language choice while your own text stays as entered. |
| Faster saving and opening | We aim to shorten the wait when you save a large batch or open My data. |

In alpha.2, the health databases, saved reports, genetic files and local logs have no app-level encryption. When you configure Oura on a Mac, its credentials are stored in the keychain by default. The proposed design would also use the keychain for the future data encryption key. The scope of file and backup encryption is still to be decided. Whether you can recover data if you lose access to the key and how recovery would work are open questions. [Current storage and protection](threat-model.en.md)

## Planned

| Item | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Oura | Personal spaces support account setup and consent. We plan to make setup easier. The trial was tested with fictional data. |
| Garmin | Today you can run read-only setup checks. A personal connection is planned. |
| Whoop | Today you can run read-only setup checks. A personal connection is planned. |
| Windows | We plan to bring the personal data space to Windows. |
| Backup and recovery | We plan to make it easier to restore a space after a mistake or when moving to another computer. |
| Export and migration | We plan a one-click CSV export so you can take your data with you. FHIR is a possible format for the longer term. |
| Accessibility | We plan to make the main tasks easier to complete with a screen reader or keyboard. |

## Exploring

| Idea | What it could offer |
| --- | --- |
| Genetic data and open-source algorithms | We are exploring local tools to organize and display genetic data, with disease risk interpretation outside the scope. |
| Body models from phone photos | We are exploring models that process photos entirely on the device for personal use, with no diagnostic purpose. |

See the [README](../../README.en.md#known-issues) for measurements with fictional data at three sizes. Genetic imports and lab report recognition were checked with fictional samples.
