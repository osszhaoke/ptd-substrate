## Personal setup and authorization
Oura is unconfigured by default. Copy the setup prompt from the device page. Check the space selected at installation. Use your own application and a loopback callback. Enter the secret in Terminal yourself for storage in the keychain. Back up the settings before changing public fields. Confirm any required restart after reviewing its effect.

## Check the connection
Sign in and consent in your browser. Then check the connection state and last sync time. Real access contacts the provider only after a separate decision by you.
